05 / 2026–2035 outlook

Earn each next step.

Product and business milestones are hypotheses. Advance when customer value, detection quality and safe operations support it.

2026–27 / VALIDATE

Focused MVP

Inventory, narrow config checks, evidence and a cloud + repository integration. Interview 20–25 teams; secure design partners and paid pilots.

2027–28 / REPEAT

Workflow & retention

Attack paths, remediation tracking, scheduled scans and multi-tenant foundations. Prove onboarding and repeat use.

2028–30 / EXPAND

More integrations

Network sources, policy-as-code, MSP workflows and AI-agent inventory and permission posture.

2030–32 / EXTEND

Cross-domain view

Correlate cloud, identity, network, APIs and software supply chain; grow selectively through partners.

2032–35 / MATURE

Policy-aware assistant

Controlled remediation with approval and rollback; broader AI governance only if trust and demand hold.

90-DAY GATE Proceed when customers pay, trust findings, onboard repeatedly and identify clear value. Narrow or pivot if only one module earns demand.
06 / Competitive reality

Differentiate through trust.

NETRA enters a mature category. A focused workflow, reliable evidence and practical adoption are a stronger opening than broad feature-parity claims.

Direct benchmark

Titania Nipper / InfraSight

Offline exported-configuration assessment and compliance evidence. Offline operation alone is not unique.

Policy & configuration

Tufin · FireMon · AlgoSec
ManageEngine · SolarWinds

Policy management, change governance, backup and compliance workflows, with differing product scope.

Cloud & exposure

Wiz · Prisma Cloud · Tenable
Qualys · Rapid7 · Orca

Adjacent cloud posture and exposure budgets. Integrate signals where useful; avoid claiming feature parity.

Deployment option

Local or private deployment can be validated.

The SIH deck proposes an air-gapped auditor and Merkle/Besu proof ledger. Later research cautions that offline operation alone is not defensible. Keep these as buyer-tested options.

Claim discipline

Measure before marketing.

The deck’s scan-speed, rule-count, time-saved, savings and readiness figures are concept claims. Prove parser coverage and outcomes in labelled pilots first. Control mapping is not certification or government endorsement.

SECURITY PRINCIPLES Read-only first · least privilege · source-backed findings · explicit approval for production changes · rollback and audit trail. Static configuration cannot prove every live control or regulatory obligation.

Security assurance that makes infrastructure risk easier to understand, prioritise and address.
